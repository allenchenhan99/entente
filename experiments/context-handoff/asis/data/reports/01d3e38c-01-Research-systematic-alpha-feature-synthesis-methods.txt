**Alpha/Feature Synthesis — Four Approaches**

**1. Genetic programming / RL formulaic alpha mining.** Composes primitives (`ts_rank`, `delta`, `decay_linear`, `correlation`, `covariance`, `signedpower`, cross-sectional `rank`, `scale`) into expression trees over price/volume/fundamental time series — the WorldQuant Alpha101 lineage. GP evolves trees via mutation/crossover, scored by IC against forward returns; **AlphaGen** ([GitHub](https://github.com/RL-MLDM/alphagen/), KDD 2023) replaced blind GP search with RL (maskable PPO) where the policy incrementally builds an expression tree and the reward is the resulting alpha's IC (or a pooled-alpha combination's IC), with `calc_mutual_IC()` explicitly penalizing alphas redundant with ones already in the pool — this pool-level mutual-IC gate is the main defense against harvesting near-duplicate factors. Requires: point-in-time price/volume/fundamental panels reshaped into Qlib-style (time × instrument) arrays. Newer variants: **AlphaForge** (dynamic re-weighting of a mined pool over time), **alpha-gfn** (GFlowNet sampling for diversity), **AlphaPROBE** (2026, graph-biased evolution), **AlphaEval** (2026, backtest-free evaluation via IC/nDCG surrogates so thousands of candidates can be screened before touching a real backtest). Pitfall: this is exactly the regime the **deflated Sharpe ratio** (Bailey & López de Prado, [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2460551); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deflated_Sharpe_ratio)) was built for — evaluating one alpha out of N trials without correcting for N is guaranteed data-snooping. Tooling is real and open-source (`gplearn`, `gpquant`, `alphagen`), not vaporware.

**2. LLM-driven alpha generation.** AlphaAgent ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.16789v2), KDD 2025) explicitly targets **alpha decay**: an LLM proposes formulas grounded in stated economic hypotheses (not just fitting), a regularizer penalizes formulas too similar to known/decayed factors, and a fitness step filters by IC plus novelty. **Cognitive Alpha Mining via LLM-Driven Code-Based Evolution** ([arXiv 2511.18850](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.18850), 2025) has the LLM emit and iteratively rewrite executable code rather than symbolic formulas. Human-in-the-loop is minimal at generation time (the LLM proposes and self-critiques) but the economic-rationale requirement is itself a soft human-designed constraint. Needs: same raw panel as (1), plus a natural-language description of each field so the LLM can reason about what it means.

**3. Deep Feature Synthesis for relational tables.** Featuretools' DFS ([docs](https://docs.featuretools.com/en/v0.16.0/automated_feature_engineering/afe.html)) is the right tool for *non-time-series* relational data — it walks entity relationships (e.g., company → insider filings → transactions) and stacks aggregation primitives (count/sum/mean/std/trend, "time since last X") automatically, no time-series operators needed. Its flagship result is fraud detection (54% fewer false positives from auto-generated aggregates like "time since last transaction"), which maps directly onto tables shaped like insider trades or 13F holdings. Pitfall: combinatorial explosion — depth-2 DFS over many tables produces thousands of correlated features, so it *must* feed into (4) or a strict multiple-testing gate, not straight into a model.

**4. Cross-sectional residual/surprise features.** Turn a raw level into "abnormal vs. expectation": actual-minus-consensus (earnings/analyst-revision surprise), or rank against ~13,000 XBRL filers rather than 500 S&P peers for a much larger reference distribution. Fama-MacBeth orthogonalization ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fama%E2%80%93MacBeth_regression); [orthogonalized-factors paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1062976913000185)) residualizes a new candidate against known factors so its reported IC reflects only incremental signal, not restated exposure to size/value/momentum you already have. A 2026 paper ([arXiv 2606.29290](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.29290)) extends this line to LLM-embedding-based textual signals propagated through supply chains — same residualization logic applied to unstructured text.

**Synthesis — do this next, in this order:**
- **Angle 4 first, it's nearly free.** You already have consensus estimates, ratings, and (via SEC XBRL frames) ~13,000-filer cross-sections per tag per quarter. Computing "surprise vs. consensus" and "percentile vs. all filers" from data you already store is hours of work, already PIT-safe by construction (same acceptedDate discipline you're already using), and directly answers "synthesize from existing data."
- **Angle 3 (DFS) second**, targeted specifically at your under-mined relational tables — insider trades, 13F holdings, executive comp, congressional trades — where you have raw event rows but only hand-picked one or two aggregates today. Highest feature-count-per-hour-of-work, but gate the output hard with IC + mutual-correlation screening before anything reaches a model.
- **Angle 1 (GP/RL) is the highest ceiling but the highest engineering cost** — it needs a proper backtest-free eval loop (AlphaEval-style) and a deflated-Sharpe or Harvey-Liu-Zhu-style multiple-testing correction wired in from day one, or the whole exercise just manufactures overfit factors faster. Don't start this until (4) and (3) are exhausted.
- **Angle 2 (LLM) is a force-multiplier on top of 1 or 3, not a standalone angle** — its real contribution is generating economically-motivated candidates faster and filtering by novelty vs. your existing factor pool, so slot it in as the proposal step once you have a mined-factor pool and eval harness to check candidates against.
- **Non-negotiable regardless of angle**: every synthesized feature needs the same PIT/no-lookahead multi-cutoff test already standard in this pipeline, and every batch of candidates needs a multiple-testing correction (deflated Sharpe or a Harvey-Liu-Zhu-style t-stat hurdle) before any single one is trusted — synthesis methods that generate hundreds of candidates make data-snooping the default failure mode, not an edge case.
