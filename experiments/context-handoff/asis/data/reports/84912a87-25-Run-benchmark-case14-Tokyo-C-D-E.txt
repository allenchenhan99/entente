## Summary — Case 14 (Tokyo, 3 days, C/D/E)

**TripGenie**: nailed it. Day1=2 (上野公園+阿美橫丁), Day2=2 (淺草寺+秋葉原電器街), Day3=0. Must-visit (上野公園) covered 1/1, placed on arrival day. Gave itemized reasons for excluding all 3 unused candidates plus an alternate itinerary variant.

**Traivial** (real `build_itinerary()` + `apply_live_constraints()`, both clocks set): Day1=2, Day2=2, Day3=0 — capacity and boundary-day rules technically obeyed. But **上野公園 (must-visit) was NOT scheduled, despite being inside the fixed stay area** — must-visit coverage 0/1. Root cause confirmed by isolating `build_itinerary()`: it geo-clustered 上野公園 with 谷中銀座 onto Day 3 purely by nearest-neighbor distance (no awareness of `must_visit` or of the upcoming departure-day wipe); `apply_live_constraints()` then correctly wiped Day 3 for the 11:00 flight, taking the must-visit down with it. This case also newly stacks capacity truncation (7 candidates vs. 6 slots) on top, silently dropping 東京國立博物館 before the clock constraint even applied — two independent, uncommunicated loss mechanisms in one case.

Key finding for the supervisor's specific question: **geographic overlap with the stay area does NOT protect a must-visit** — the clustering algorithm never checks `must_visit` status, so proximity is irrelevant to whether the item lands on a day later wiped by boundary constraints. This is the same architectural gap as case11, now verified under the most favorable possible geographic condition and shown to still fail.
