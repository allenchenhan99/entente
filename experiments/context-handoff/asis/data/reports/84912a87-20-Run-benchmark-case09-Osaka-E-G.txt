Both systems handled the core oracle correctly: Shinsaibashi (G test — 6-min pause outside one store, not a real visit) stayed schedulable on both sides, Kuromon Market (must-visit) was covered by both, hotel/base stayed in Namba, and neither scheduled anything past 12:00 on day 4.

- Traivial: 0 leakage(n/a), coverage 1/1 (Kuromon got lucky, landed on unaffected Day 2), Day 4 = 0 stops (even cleaner match to oracle than TripGenie), but **1 real infeasibility-adjacent defect**, ≥1 manual fix needed, partial evidence, UNKNOWN-handling over-conservative.

**Key finding**: Running the real harness (not just reading code) confirmed Day 1 was entirely wiped. Root cause: leaving `outbound_arrival` unset (correct per this case's spec — no arrival constraint) makes `is_arrival_day = (arrival_at is None and index==0)` true, and `_constrain_day()` then blanks day 1 with "arrival-day flight evidence missing." This silently dropped Universal Studios Japan and Abeno Harukas (never named in caveats — only the day-level note). This is the exact mirror of case08's departure-day bug, now confirmed on the arrival side too, indicating a systemic design flaw in `live_constraints.py`'s `is_arrival_day`/`is_departure_day` defaults rather than a one-off.
